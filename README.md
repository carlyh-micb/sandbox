## 1. Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the standardized process for preparing a Peanut Butter and Jelly (PB&J) sandwich. Following this procedure ensures consistent quality, food safety, and customer satisfaction.

---

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all personnel responsible for preparing PB&J sandwiches in home, educational, or commercial kitchen environments.

---

## 3. Ingredients

The following ingredients are required for one sandwich:

- 2 slices of bread, specify type
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter
- 1 tablespoon jelly or jam
- Optional:
  - Honey
  - Banana slices
  - Strawberry slices
  - Salt and peppers
---

## 4. Materials Required

- Butter knife or spreader
- Cutting board or plate
- Measuring spoons (optional)
- Serving plate
- Napkin

---

## 5. Safety Considerations

> **Warning:** Verify that no individuals consuming the sandwich have peanut or tree nut allergies.

### Hygiene Requirements

1. Wash hands thoroughly before handling food.
2. Wear gloves (optional).
3. Ensure all preparation surfaces are clean.
4. Use clean utensils during preparation.

---

## 6. Procedure

### Step 1: Prepare the Workspace

1. Gather all ingredients and materials.
2. Clean the preparation area.
3. Place the two bread slices on a clean plate or cutting board.

### Step 2: Apply Peanut Butter

1. Open the peanut butter container.
2. Measure approximately 2 tablespoons of peanut butter.
3. Spread evenly across one slice of bread.

### Step 3: Apply Jelly

1. Open the jelly container.
2. Measure approximately 1 tablespoon of jelly.
3. Spread evenly across the second slice of bread.

### Step 4: Assemble the Sandwich

1. Align both slices of bread.
2. Press the peanut butter side and jelly side together.
3. Apply gentle pressure to secure the sandwich.

Optional: Distribute fruit slices or honey on one slice of bread before pressing slices together.

### Step 5: Serve

1. Slice the sandwich in half if desired.
2. Place on a serving plate.
3. Serve immediately.

---

## 7. Quality Control Checklist

Before serving, confirm that:

- [ ] Peanut butter is spread evenly
- [ ] Jelly is spread evenly
- [ ] Bread is not torn
- [ ] Sandwich is fully assembled
- [ ] Work area remains clean

---

## 8. Troubleshooting

### Problem: Jelly leaks out of the sandwich

**Possible Cause:** Excessive jelly applied.

**Solution:** Reduce the amount of jelly and spread more evenly.

### Problem: Bread tears during spreading

**Possible Cause:** Bread is too soft or pressure was excessive.

**Solution:** Use gentle spreading motions or firmer bread.

---

## 9. Records

No records are required for routine home preparation. Commercial environments may log:

- Date prepared
- Preparer's name
- Batch quantity
- Quality inspection results
